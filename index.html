Core Mechanics & Scoring Mathematics

Incoming colored blocks slide inwards along the six lanes of the hexagon. When a block touches the central core or already placed blocks, it snaps into place. When 3 or more adjacent blocks of the identical color connect (either vertically along the same edge or horizontally across adjacent edges), they detonate and disappear!

Points are calculated via an exponential formula:
Score = (Blocks Destroyed)² × Combo Multiplier × 10
Triggering consecutive clears before the combo gauge runs dry activates x2, x3, x4, up to x10 Multipliers, catapulting your score from hundreds into tens of thousands!

💡 5 Pro Strategies to Score 50,000+ in Hextris

1. Maintain Even Lane Heights

Never allow one lane to stack up high while others remain empty. An uneven tower quickly reaches the danger boundary, leaving you with zero room for error during speed surges.

2. Build Adjacent Color Bridges

Remember that blocks connect across lane borders! Placing two yellow blocks on lane 1 and one yellow block on lane 2 triggers a match, allowing smart horizontal clears.

3. Leverage the Combo Timer

When blocks clear, you have roughly 3 seconds to land another match. Chaining clears keeps your combo multiplier elevated, which is the only way to break leaderboard records.

4. Anticipate Multi-Block Ring Waves

As the difficulty climbs, Hextris will spawn rings with 5 blocks and a single opening. Always identify the gap immediately and orient your most vulnerable side towards the opening!
